---
name: source-discovery
description: Find diverse, relevant candidate sources with durable discovery metadata.
---

Use varied search angles. Prefer primary sources, official data, and original
research. For every candidate, record title, URL, snippet, search rationale,
and source family. Use `ddg_web_search` to find candidates, then use
`web_fetch` to retrieve the relevant public page before treating it as support.
Discovery is not evidence: do not make unsupported factual claims and do not
treat a search snippet as verified support.
