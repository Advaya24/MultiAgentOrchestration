# Selected Engineering Focus

The solution will go deep on two areas from the assignment brief.

## 1. Orchestration and Handoffs Across Agents

Focus on a research work item from assignment through completion: what it asks for, which durable artifact it produces, and how a downstream worker learns it is ready. The walkthrough should also cover a worker failure and reassignment.

## 2. Context Management as the Window Fills

Focus on the bounded context supplied to each research step and the compact, traceable artifact it leaves behind. The walkthrough should show how a late contradiction causes focused revision without reloading the whole run.

## Supporting, Not Primary, Areas

Persistent state supports handoffs and context selection. Planning/revision, recovery, and tool execution will be implemented only as needed to demonstrate the two chosen areas.

## Deferred Discussion

The specific worker topology, storage design, provider choice, and recovery mechanics remain solution-phase decisions.
